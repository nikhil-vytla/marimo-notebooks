A small Three.js scaffold with orbit controls and one distortion parameter. The scene is intentionally simple: a wireframe shell, a point cloud, and a camera the reader can move.

Controls

The page uses OrbitControls and a single BufferGeometry. The distortion is applied in the vertex shader through a uniform, so the geometry does not rebuild on every change.

If the CDN is blocked, the fallback is visible and the page still loads. That is the minimum bar for a 3D reference.
